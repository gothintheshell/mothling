First of all, you may have noticed a new format on the site, I decided that any of my random lists of songs or things I love might as well just be a part of the main blog. 

So here we are.👻

As a Scorpio, Autumn has always felt like my season, even if this one is a bit different.

This year, I'm focusing on enjoying what I can. The following list are a few of the things I'm in love with right now: 

* Fenty Beauty Gloass Bomb Stix in Midnight Ballerina. Strangely, I didn't realise the pop culture reference in the shade's name, but it's a beautiful dark plum that's perfect for a gothic look. 
![Close up of my lips wearing a dark plum, almost black, lipgloss and my gold septum ring in my nose. In the bottom right is a heart shaped frame of a photo of the product boxing showing the shade name, Midnight Ballerina.](/images/midnight-ballerina.jpg)

* Vans Knu Skools, comfort and they make my larger feet look small. I now own 3 pair: Classic Checkerboard, Platform light blue, and black and a metallic silver colour that looks more like gunmetal in person.
![Sitting on a brown couch with my left leg crossed over my right knee to show a new pair of light blue platform Vans shoes. I'm also wearing a black skirt and black ruffle socks.](/images/knu-blue.jpg)  

![A pair of black Vans shoes with a metallic silver stripe with black speckles and white soles resting on a brown couch with a a number of pillows and a blanket in the background.](/images/vans-knu.jpg)

* Holler Glaze Over in Cocoa Donut, though I really want to try the Cinnamon Swirl scent because I love cinnamon. The glaze itself is so comfortable on my lips and they smell delicious.  

* Speaking of cinnamon, my Starface Lip Balm in Cheeky Churro. They stopped selling that scent here, so I picked up some more when I was in the States. 

* Slouchy socks, I only just bought a pair and now I’m going to live in them all Autumn and Winter, and maybe buy them some friends!  

* My Orly nail polish in Rollin With My Homies, a purple with lime chrome shimmer, circa 2000s.
