August was an unusual month, and it meant less time spent finding new music and less time available for listening to the music I do have. So this month's list is going to be a bit shorter, but here we go: 

1. [Milo J - Bajo De La Piel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GCyD6HexgM), what more can I say, there's just something about this song and the emotions conveyed within it.   
2. [Fat Dog](https://fatdogfatdogfatdog.bandcamp.com/music), my obsession with this band continues.  
3. [Grandma's House - Baby You're a Winner (Album)](https://grandmashouseband.bandcamp.com/album/baby-youre-a-winner), I love their sound so much and the videos that go along with it. Two of my favourites are Blue Oblivion and Dance.  
4. [The Toadies - Possum Kingdom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EkwD5rQ-_d4) an old favourite, like putting on a comfortable cardigan.  
5. [greek - HOLD ME](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rdJNlJTtsOY) came across this one as a suggestion, and it makes me want to dance. Hold me like  you need me.
