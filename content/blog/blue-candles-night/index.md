# The Night the Candles Burned Blue

I woke up last night with the distinct memory of a room filled with smoke, but the air was cool. In the center of the room, a cluster of candles burned with a flame that wasn't orange, but a deep, electric blue.

![Blue candles on a dark table](blue-candles.jpg)

*The candles seemed to hum, a low vibration that I felt in my teeth rather than heard.*

I reached out to touch one, expecting the heat, but my hand passed through the flame as if it were made of water. The blue light reflected in my eyes, turning the world into a monochrome of indigo and silver.

## The Meaning

Was it a warning? Or a promise? In the dream, the blue fire didn't consume; it revealed. It showed the shadows behind the walls, the secrets hidden in the dust.

I woke before I could see what lay behind the shadows.
