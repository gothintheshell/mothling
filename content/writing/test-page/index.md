<div class="poem-stanza">the streetlamp hums above me<br>
flickering in and out<br>
like a signal I can't quite receive</div>

<div class="poem-stanza"><br>

i am learning to read the gaps<br>
between what's said<br>
and what's held close to the chest</div>

<div class="poem-stanza"><br>

there is beauty in the static<br>
in the things left unsaid<br>
in the quiet spaces where<br>
light bends around obstacles</div>

<!--more-->

### Notes on this piece

Written late at night, walking home from somewhere I needed to leave. The streetlamps were out in half the neighborhood — just enough darkness to see the stars properly.
